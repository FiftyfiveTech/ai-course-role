# Learning Plan — soda-1000

## question_coverage
Review the product features and typical customer questions to ensure comprehensive coverage. Practice summarizing key points in concise responses.

## listening_ratio
Focus on active listening by pausing after each customer statement and paraphrasing to confirm understanding.

## factual_accuracy
Verify facts against up-to-date product documentation before responding. Use reliable sources to double-check information.

## policy_adherence
Revisit company policies and guidelines. Role‑play scenarios to reinforce correct application of policies.

## objection_handling
Study common objections and develop structured rebuttals. Practice using the Acknowledge‑Explain‑Confirm technique.

## clarity
Simplify language, avoid jargon, and use short sentences. Record practice sessions and review for clarity.
