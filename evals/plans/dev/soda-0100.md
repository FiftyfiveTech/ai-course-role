# Learning Plan — soda-0100

## listening_ratio
Focus on active listening by summarizing the trainee's questions before responding, ensuring that at least 50% of the conversation is spent listening rather than speaking. Practice techniques such as reflective listening and asking clarifying questions to demonstrate attentiveness.

## policy_adherence
Review the organization's policies and guidelines thoroughly. During coaching sessions, incorporate policy references explicitly when addressing trainee queries, and verify that all responses align with the latest policy documents.

## objection_handling
Develop a structured approach to objections: acknowledge the concern, provide evidence-based counterpoints, and confirm understanding. Role-play common objections to build confidence and improve response time.

## clarity
Simplify explanations by using concise language, avoiding jargon, and checking for understanding with quick comprehension checks. Aim to keep sentences short and focused on the main point.
