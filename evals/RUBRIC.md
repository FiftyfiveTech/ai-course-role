# ROLE Evaluation Rubric

Each item is scored **0 / 1 / 2**.  
The transcript evidence column states exactly what text in the transcript proves the score — if the evidence cannot be located, the item scores 0.

---

## Item 1 — Question Coverage

**What it measures:** Did the trainee ask the questions needed to fully understand the customer's problem before proposing a solution?

| Score | Transcript evidence required |
|-------|------------------------------|
| 2 | Trainee asks at least 2 clarifying questions (order number, desired change, timeline, etc.) before stating any resolution. Both questions appear verbatim in the trainee turns. |
| 1 | Trainee asks exactly 1 clarifying question before proposing a resolution. |
| 0 | Trainee proposes a resolution with zero clarifying questions, or first clarifying question appears after a resolution is already offered. |

---

## Item 2 — Listening Ratio

**What it measures:** Did the trainee acknowledge and reflect the customer's stated concern before speaking about process or policy?

| Score | Transcript evidence required |
|-------|------------------------------|
| 2 | Trainee turn immediately following the customer's problem statement contains a direct paraphrase or acknowledgement of the customer's specific concern (e.g. order number, product name, or complaint keyword) within the first sentence. |
| 1 | Trainee acknowledges the concern but only after first stating policy or process steps. |
| 0 | No acknowledgement of the customer's specific concern appears anywhere in trainee turns. |

---

## Item 3 — Factual Accuracy

**What it measures:** Did the trainee state only facts that are consistent with the scenario's policy document and the customer's stated details?

| Score | Transcript evidence required |
|-------|------------------------------|
| 2 | Every factual claim in trainee turns (prices, order IDs, product names, timelines) matches either the scenario policy file or a detail provided by the customer in the same session. No invented figures. |
| 1 | At most one minor factual inconsistency (e.g. rounding a price) that does not affect the resolution. |
| 0 | Any invented order ID, price, product name, or timeline that was not stated by the customer or the policy. |

---

## Item 4 — Policy Adherence

**What it measures:** Did the trainee follow the scenario policy (e.g. confirm before acting, escalate when required)?

| Score | Transcript evidence required |
|-------|------------------------------|
| 2 | Every action the trainee proposes (change, refund, escalation) is preceded by an explicit confirmation request to the customer ("Shall I go ahead and…?") and the customer's agreement appears in the next turn. |
| 1 | Trainee confirms at least once but skips confirmation for one action. |
| 0 | Trainee commits to an action with no confirmation request, or takes an action the policy prohibits. |

---

## Item 5 — Objection Handling

**What it measures:** When the customer pushes back or expresses frustration, did the trainee address the objection directly rather than repeating the same response?

| Score | Transcript evidence required |
|-------|------------------------------|
| 2 | After any customer objection or expression of frustration, the trainee's next turn introduces a new piece of information, an alternative, or an escalation path not mentioned in previous trainee turns. |
| 1 | Trainee responds to the objection but repeats at least one full sentence from a prior trainee turn verbatim. |
| 0 | Trainee response to an objection is a verbatim or near-verbatim repeat of the immediately preceding trainee turn, or the objection is ignored entirely. |

---

## Item 6 — Clarity

**What it measures:** Did the trainee state the resolution and next steps in plain language without jargon or ambiguity?

| Score | Transcript evidence required |
|-------|------------------------------|
| 2 | The final trainee turn before session end contains: (a) a single sentence naming the specific action taken or to be taken, and (b) a concrete next step for the customer (e.g. "You will receive a confirmation email within 24 hours"). Both (a) and (b) are present. |
| 1 | Final trainee turn contains either (a) or (b) but not both. |
| 0 | Final trainee turn ends without stating what was done or what happens next. |

---

## Scoring summary

| Item | Max |
|------|-----|
| 1 Question Coverage | 2 |
| 2 Listening Ratio | 2 |
| 3 Factual Accuracy | 2 |
| 4 Policy Adherence | 2 |
| 5 Objection Handling | 2 |
| 6 Clarity | 2 |
| **Total** | **12** |

Pass threshold: **≥ 8 / 12** (67 %).

---

## Deletion rule

Any rubric item whose evidence column cannot be answered by reading the transcript alone is deleted in the next revision, not softened. Vague criteria (e.g. "seemed empathetic") are not permitted.
