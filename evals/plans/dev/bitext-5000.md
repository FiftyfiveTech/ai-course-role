# Learning Plan — bitext-5000

## question_coverage
Review the session objectives and outline responses to ensure all key questions are addressed. Practice summarizing each question before answering.

## listening_ratio
Pause briefly before responding, summarize the client’s points, and ask clarifying questions to demonstrate active listening.

## factual_accuracy
Verify facts before sharing by consulting reliable sources or double‑checking data points during preparation.

## policy_adherence
Familiarize yourself with the organization’s policies and rehearse responses that align with policy requirements, avoiding conflicting content.

## objection_handling
Use a structured approach: acknowledge the objection, clarify the concern, and provide evidence‑based solutions. Practice common objection scenarios.

## clarity
Simplify language, use short sentences, and repeat key points. Speak slowly and check for understanding.
