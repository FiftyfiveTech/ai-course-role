# Learning Plan — hh-80002

## question_coverage
Review the session objectives and ensure all key topics are addressed. Use a checklist to confirm each question aligns with the learning goals.

## listening_ratio
Practice active listening by summarizing the trainee's statements before responding, and monitor the proportion of time spent listening versus speaking.

## factual_accuracy
Verify all factual statements against reliable sources before the session, and incorporate a brief fact-checking step in your preparation.

## policy_adherence
Familiarize yourself with the latest policy guidelines and incorporate them into your responses; review policy documents before each session.

## objection_handling
Develop a structured objection-handling framework, rehearse common objections, and practice responding calmly and confidently.

## clarity
Use concise language, avoid jargon, and pause to confirm understanding; rehearse key points to ensure clear delivery.
