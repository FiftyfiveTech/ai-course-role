# Learning Plan — hh-80012

## question_coverage
Review the session outline to ensure all key topics are addressed. Use a checklist to confirm each major point is covered before moving on.

## listening_ratio
Practice active listening by summarizing the trainee’s questions before responding. Aim to spend at least 60% of the conversation listening.

## factual_accuracy
Verify facts with reputable sources before sharing. Keep a reference list handy and double-check any statistics or claims.

## policy_adherence
Familiarize yourself with the organization’s policies and guidelines. Rehearse responses to common policy questions to ensure compliance.

## objection_handling
Develop a set of standard rebuttals for common objections. Practice responding calmly and providing evidence to support your position.

## clarity
Use simple, concise language. Avoid jargon and check for understanding by asking the trainee to paraphrase.
